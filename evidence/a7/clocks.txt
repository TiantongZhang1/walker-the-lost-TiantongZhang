  WHAT THIS MEASURES, AND WHERE CHANGE-BRIEF.md SECTION 8 WAS WRONG

  Section 8 predicted the idle figure would be UP TO ONE PHYSICS
  STEP LATE. Measured, it is one physics step EARLY, and the cause
  is not read latency at all. An idle-mode mixer gets an extra
  advance during the idle step of the frame the clip STARTS -- a
  step the physics-mode mixer does not get until the next frame.
  So the idle clip runs one step ahead of the physics clip for the
  whole of its length.

  Section 8's actual CLAIM survives, and is what the first two
  checks establish: the window opens on the frame the playhead
  reaches 0.2000, in both modes. Writer and reader share a tick.
  The disagreement between the clip's write and gameplay's read
  is ZERO SECONDS, which is what shipping on physics buys.

NOTE  WHAT THIS RUN CANNOT SEE. `--fixed-fps` locks the render tick to the physics tick, so the idle step always falls between the same
NOTE  two physics steps and every run of this file is identical. Section 8 also predicts the idle figure JITTERS between runs, and
NOTE  THAT is where idle's real unreliability lives: at a free frame rate the idle step wanders. It needs a windowed run and is NOT
NOTE  established here. The SIZE of the offset is measured; its VARIANCE is not. Running this three times under a locked clock
NOTE  would be one measurement repeated, not three measurements.
