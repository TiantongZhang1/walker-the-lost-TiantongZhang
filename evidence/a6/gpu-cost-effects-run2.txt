  So the per-effect figures are NOT separable at this scale.
  A set of conditions whose larger one measures cheaper than a
  subset of itself is not reporting the price of an effect; it
  is reporting the floor of the instrument. Each run reproduces
  itself to the last digit, which is what makes this worth
  saying out loud -- repeatable is not the same as true.
